About the System

InfoTranSys is a web-based transaction management system developed to modernize and improve the services of the University Cashier's Office and Dean's Offices by reducing long waiting lines, minimizing manual processes, and providing a faster and more organized transaction experience for students and university personnel. The system serves as a centralized platform where students can conveniently submit requests, monitor the status of their transactions, and receive updates without the need to repeatedly visit university offices.

What it does

InfoTranSys provides dedicated dashboards for Cashier and Dean's Office staff to efficiently manage incoming requests, verify submitted documents, process transactions, and update request statuses. The system also offers organized record management, search and filtering capabilities, transaction history, and reporting features that help improve office productivity and reduce administrative workload. By eliminating repetitive manual tasks and centralizing transaction records, the platform enables staff to handle a larger volume of requests more efficiently while maintaining accurate and secure data.

What it brings

The platform digitizes various office transactions by allowing students to access services online, reducing paperwork and improving communication between students and administrative staff. Through an intuitive and user-friendly interface, students can submit transaction requests, upload the necessary documents, track the progress of their requests in real time, and receive notifications once their transactions have been processed or require additional requirements. This significantly shortens processing time while providing greater transparency throughout the entire transaction process. It also facilitates better coordination between departments, and provides a modern user experience for both students and administrative staff.
