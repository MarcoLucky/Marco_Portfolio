StartUp QC

Quezon City, Philippines

About the Event

Startup QC is a student competition event held in Quezon City that brings together young innovators and entrepreneurs to showcase their ideas and startup concepts. This event provided an excellent opportunity to network with fellow students, learn from industry professionals, and present innovative solutions to real-world problems. Participating in this competition helped me develop my pitching skills, gain confidence in public speaking, and understand the startup ecosystem better.

What I Gained

Through this competition, I strengthened my problem-solving abilities, learned to think creatively about business solutions, and developed teamwork and collaboration skills. The experience also gave me valuable insights into entrepreneurship, market validation, and the importance of user-focused design. These learnings continue to influence my approach to technology projects and my vision as an IT professional.
